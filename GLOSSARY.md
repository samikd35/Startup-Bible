# Startup Bible

Startup Bible helps founders use discovery evidence to decide what to try and what to build.

## Language

**Discovery evidence**:
A sourced observation about a problem, workflow, workaround, or outcome in the target domain.

**Hypothesis**:
A testable claim about a problem or how a proposed change might address it.

**Pilot**:
A time-limited trial with one customer, a defined scope, and a measurable outcome.

**Roadmap recommendation**:
A proposal to change the shared product for a target group of customers.

**Product owner**:
The founder or designated person with authority to approve what the team builds.

**Customer sponsor**:
The person authorized to commit the customer's time, data, or other resources to a pilot.

**Project journal**:
The record of recommendations, their evidence, the decisions they led to, and known outcomes.

**Background guidance**:
An optional recommendation or focused question about what to test or build, prompted by new discovery evidence.

**Checkpoint**:
A point in project work when Startup Bible considers whether new evidence warrants guidance.

**Skill maintainer**:
The person authorized to approve changes to the shared Startup Bible workflow.
